# SA-09 — Engagement duration — 2026-10-26

Summary of supplied discussion; quoted author excerpts and agreed paragraph are verbatim. Not a full transcript.

The assistant initially proposed establishing vision, decision-making, production changes and succession. The author challenged the succession assumption:

> Ну, подготовить передачу преемнику, это, конечно, звучит хорошо, а кто преемник

The assistant withdrew mandatory succession. The author proposed a minimum year rather than 12–36 months, explaining that understanding the project, domain, organizational politics and engineers takes time before improvements can be delivered. The minimum is an author recommendation, not externally verified research.

## Final proposed paragraph

> The most realistic approach is to engage a senior Solutions Architect from outside the company. Plan for at least 12 months: the architect needs time to understand the business, domain, solution, and team, work through organizational constraints, and carry improvements through implementation into production.
