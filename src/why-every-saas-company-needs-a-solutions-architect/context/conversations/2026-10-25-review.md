## Assistant discussion — summary, not verbatim

The initial review praised Demand = Business + Domain and Capability = Software + Infrastructure, the progression from local decisions to missing ownership, and the requirement to stay through implementation. It challenged absolute claims about distributing work, growing an internal architect, architect tenure, vendor loyalty and a contractor-only acquisition model. It suggested distinguishing architectural function from job title and explaining experience in terms of demonstrated judgment rather than exact years. It also identified the typo “Infrastructure realtiy”.

After the author's objections, the assistant withdrew “almost impossible” as a characterization of the experience profile. The relevant issue became whether the article describes an excellent senior architect or imposes rigid minimum year thresholds. The author is not to be described as unique or impossible.

The vision discussion distinguished delegating activities from holding an integrated model and final architectural accountability. Several architects can contribute. Combining partial competence does not automatically fill the integration gap.

The internal-development discussion identified two mechanisms: promotion does not create missing experience, and development requires a qualified mentor plus exposure to varied decisions and consequences. Mature consulting organizations can supply those conditions; unsupported exclusivity claims still need care.

The acquisition discussion retained the author's 12–36 month external-SA model as a credible way to establish capability. Suggested outcomes included vision, principles, governance, target state, implementation continuity and development/recruitment of a successor. Permanent hiring and a consultancy with a named accountable architect were other proposed routes. The architect need not automatically depart after 36 months.

The assistant produced SA-01–SA-18 and grouped them into five working blocks. The proposed per-item process was proposal → reasoning → author decision → agreed text. No blanket approval of that backlog was supplied.
