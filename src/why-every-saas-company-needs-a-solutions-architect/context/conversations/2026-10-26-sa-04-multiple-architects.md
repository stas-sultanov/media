## Proposal — summary and verbatim wording

After SA-03 was committed, the author requested the next improvement. The assistant introduced SA-04: clarify that a large product may have several Solutions Architects while accountability for the complete solution remains clearly owned.

The proposed sentence was:

> Large products may have more than one Solutions Architect, but accountability for the end-to-end solution must be clearly owned.

The assistant proposed placing it after the SA-03 committee paragraph, before the section's concluding statements, and invited review of its placement.

The assistant read the section “How does the SA do this in practice?” and marked the proposed sentence after the committee paragraph. The supplied playback lists the six process step names without their detailed descriptions; the reviewed ending below preserves the relevant insertion context.

> A committee of partial views does not create one complete mind; they lack shared context and unified accountability.
>
> Large products may have more than one Solutions Architect, but accountability for the end-to-end solution must be clearly owned.

The multiple-architects sentence is the proposed addition. The other sentences above were already present at [commit f4b0000](https://github.com/stas-sultanov/media/blob/f4b0000eec107e014d4ab5678ae09ff1a79cf006/src/why-every-saas-company-needs-a-solutions-architect/article.md).

## Outcome — summary

The author approved the exact proposed sentence in the reviewed location. It is implemented as a separate paragraph after SA-03 in [article.md](../../article.md). The rest of the article is preserved.

The purpose is to distinguish the number of contributing architects from accountability for the whole solution, while retaining the unified-vision direction agreed in SA-02. Only SA-04 is resolved in this change, following the agreed one-commit-per-SA-item workflow.
