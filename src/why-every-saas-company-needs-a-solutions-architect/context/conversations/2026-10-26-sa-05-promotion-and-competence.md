# SA-05 — Promotion and competence — 2026-10-26

Record of the article-related SA-05 discussion supplied in this session. Quoted author statements and English proposals are verbatim; connecting explanations are summaries. This is not a complete transcript of every message or tool action.

## Initial proposal — summary and verbatim wording

After SA-04, the author asked what was next. The assistant introduced SA-05: promotion provides a title and authority but does not create missing experience.

The initial proposed sentence was:

> Promoting an internal engineer gives them title and authority, but not the missing experience across other systems, domains, and technologies.

The assistant initially referred to “Who can play this role?” while also naming the internal-promotion discussion. In the canonical source, that discussion is the subsection “3. Level up an internal employee” under “Why isn’t there an alternative?”.

## Author correction — verbatim

> Э... Меня все триггерит это вот это other systems. Э ... Дело в том, что вот берут, допустим, .NET инженера, вот, и говорят, ты сегодня ты архитектор. Так он и квалифицированным .NET инженером в своей области от этого не становится

## Revised proposal — summary and verbatim wording

The assistant acknowledged that the point also concerns competence within the person's existing specialty. Assigning an architecture title does not itself improve a .NET engineer's .NET competence. The argument should not be restricted to missing exposure to other systems, domains or technologies.

The revised sentence was:

> Promotion grants title and mandate, not competence.

The assistant proposed following this sentence with the existing explanation of how experience is accumulated.

## Author approval of wording — verbatim

> Да

The assistant then stated that SA-05 would be implemented as a separate commit with the history and rationale.

## Author confirmation of implementation — verbatim

> Да

## Placement and outcome — summary

The approved sentence is inserted as a separate paragraph in “3. Level up an internal employee”, immediately after the assumption and before the existing explanation:

> Assumption: mentor a strong internal engineer or delivery lead into an SA over time.
>
> Promotion grants title and mandate, not competence.
>
> This rarely succeeds. Correct judgment in this role comes from years of exposure to different pressures and failures:

This is the internal-promotion subsection named in the discussion, not a new paragraph in the earlier competence-profile section. The remaining article text is preserved.

Only SA-05 is resolved by this change. The discussion, approved wording, rationale and backlog status are included with the article edit in one separate SA-05 commit.
