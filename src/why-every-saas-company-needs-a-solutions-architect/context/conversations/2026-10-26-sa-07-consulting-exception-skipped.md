# SA-07 — Consulting-company caveat skipped — 2026-10-26

Record of the article-related SA-07 discussion supplied in this session. Quoted author statements and the English proposal are verbatim; connecting explanations are summaries. Repeated readings and operational acknowledgments are omitted. This is not a full transcript.

## Proposal and review — summary

After SA-06, the assistant introduced the proposal to qualify the internal-development argument with an exception for mature consulting companies. The author initially agreed to explore the addition and asked for the wording:

> Да, давай добавим. Какая оговорка, что там

The proposed addition to the end of “3. Level up an internal employee” was:

> A mature consulting company can develop an SA internally if it provides experienced SA mentors, a structured development process, and exposure to different clients, domains, and solutions.

The proposed mechanism was experienced mentors, a structured development process and varied client/project exposure. Consulting-company status alone was not presented as a guarantee. The author asked to hear the complete subsection:

> Прочитай мне всё от и до

## Reconsideration and final decision

After reviewing the proposed caveat in context, the author questioned whether it was needed:

> А вообще нам это надо

The assistant recommended omitting it: the existing “rarely” and “usually” wording already allows exceptions, and explaining architect development inside consulting companies would distract from the article's SaaS focus.

The author accepted that recommendation:

> Отлично, пропускаем и идем дальше

## Outcome — summary

SA-07 is skipped by the author's decision. The proposed English sentence is retained here as discussion history and is not added to the canonical article. This final decision supersedes the earlier invitation to explore the addition. No other backlog item is approved by it.
