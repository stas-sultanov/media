## Initial assistant proposal — summary and verbatim proposed paragraph

The assistant identified the opening of [article.md](../../article.md), which began with “A SaaS company’s business model depends entirely on the IT solution it builds and operates.” It proposed prepending this paragraph:

> Many types of IT solutions need a Solutions Architect. This article focuses on SaaS, where the company’s responsibility for both building and operating a continuously evolving solution makes architectural ownership especially important.

The stated purpose was to explain the SaaS scope and continuous architectural responsibility. The assistant advised against asserting that SaaS needs the role more than every other kind of solution, since the article does not establish that comparison.

Например, while many different types of business that runs it solutions, requires IT Solutions Architec. This article foceuses on it saas buimess, where company builds and operates IT solution, wgich makes SA role yhe most important.

## Assistant revision — verbatim substantive response

Да. Ключевой аргумент: **в SaaS само IT-решение является продуктом и основой бизнеса.**

> While many businesses that rely on IT solutions need an IT Solutions Architect, this article focuses on SaaS companies, where the IT solution itself is the product. Building and operating it is the core of the business, making the Solutions Architect’s role especially important.

**“Especially important”** точнее, чем “the most important”: последнее означает, что SA — самая важная роль в компании.

## Author approval and implementation request — verbatim

## Outcome — summary

The final English paragraph above was approved for insertion before the existing opening on 2026-10-26. The rationale is business-centered: SaaS companies build and operate the IT solution that is their product and business foundation. The wording does not rank the SA above every other company role. Only SA-01 was approved and applied in this change.
